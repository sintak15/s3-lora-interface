# Latest face with tighter CYD pilots

Full face: `hardware/enclosure/enclosure-v4-front-cyd-m25.stl`.
Small single-post test: `hardware/enclosure/enclosure-v4-cyd-m25-test.stl`.

The CYD pilots are reduced from **2.5 to 2.0 mm diameter** for M2.5 screw
engagement. Only the four CYD pilot diameters change. The posts remain
3.3 mm above their retained 2.4 mm floor, with Z=5.7 mm seating height and
42 × 78 mm mounting pitch. The blind pilot is 2.8 mm deep, beginning 0.5 mm
above the inside floor.

Use **M2.5 × 4 mm pan-head screws**, assuming a 1.6 mm PCB, rather than the
earlier M3 CYD screws. Engagement is approximately 2.4 mm, with 0.4 mm before
the pilot bottom. Check actual PCB thickness and screw length. Try the small
single-post coupon with the real screw first; tap M2.5 if needed instead of
forcing the screw and splitting the post.

This face also includes the filled blue-marked NEO-6M post rims, the complete
25 × 25 × 7.5 mm antenna stack on 3.8 mm ledges with bracket posts at 11.3 mm,
the 2.5 mm rightward antenna shift, original NEO-6M seat height, restored
Heltec bracket-post height and face-entry case-screw clearance holes.
Existing latest base and antenna brackets remain compatible.

Print exterior face down, 100% scale, 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls. Inspect the filled
NEO-6M caps for local underside-support needs. Physical fit is not verified.
