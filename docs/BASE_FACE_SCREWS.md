# Base with case screws inserted through the face

Print `enclosure-v4-base-face-screws.stl`. Source:
`enclosure_v4_base_face_screws.scad`. This retains the faster base's lightened
floor/walls, component seats and matching lip for the existing printed face.

The four 8 mm-diameter corner posts now have **2.6 mm pilot holes, 12 mm deep**,
opening at the mating seam. Rear case-screw clearance holes and head recesses
are removed; the rear floor is continuous under each post.

Drill ONLY the four case-screw positions on the existing face to **3.2–3.4 mm
clearance**, all the way through the front half. Do not enlarge the CYD or
Heltec mounting holes. Remove the electronics before drilling.

Use **four M3 × 25 mm pan-head screws** from the outside of the face. With the
nominal 16 mm front-half depth and no head recess, they engage approximately
9 mm into the base, with 3 mm remaining before the blind pilot bottom.
Washers reduce engagement by their thickness. Verify your actual printed
front depth; do not use the earlier 40 mm case screws or force a bottomed screw.
Tap the 2.6 mm pilots for M3 if needed and tighten gently in printed plastic.

Print exterior rear face down at 100% in millimetres. For a 0.4 mm nozzle,
try 0.28 mm layers, 3 walls, 3 top/bottom layers and 10% rectilinear infill.
Inspect the side USB, switch and SMA openings for local support needs.
This model has not been physically test printed or assembled.
